A scalable mobile payments app, from zero to launch

PNC · Vice President, Senior Technical Product Owner

The problem
Small merchants needed to accept card payments on their phones, inside a regulated bank.
What I did
Owned strategy, roadmap, and a $10M budget for PNC Mobile Accept on iOS and Android. Secured approval for a scalable microservices architecture with mobile SDK and payment-gateway integrations, and built in PCI-DSS and fraud controls early.

Selected work

A few products and problems I've owned, what I did, and what changed as a result.

A mobile payments app, from zero to launch

PNC · Vice President, Senior Technical Product Owner

The problem
Small and medium-sized merchants needed a simple way to accept card payments on their phones, inside a bank's regulated environment.
What I did
Owned strategy, roadmap, and a $10M delivery budget for PNC Mobile Accept on iOS and Android. Secured architecture approval for the microservices, mobile SDK, and payment-gateway integrations, and built PCI-DSS and fraud requirements in early with Risk, Compliance, and Legal.
The result
Delivered the MVP within six months. After launch, a self-apply onboarding feature drove a 30% increase in adoption, and a new card reader cut hardware cost by $140 per unit.

Restarting a stalled data platform

PNC · Merchant Data Warehouse

The problem
A Hadoop-based merchant data platform had stalled, blocked by unresolved dependencies between engineering and payment-gateway teams.
What I did
Took over product ownership, aligned the stakeholders on priorities, and set up recurring dependency reviews so blockers surfaced and got resolved quickly.
The result
Production releases began within two months.

Fixing a card-reader pairing problem

PNC · PNC Mobile Accept

The problem
iOS merchants had trouble pairing their card readers, which generated support tickets.
What I did
Introduced an in-app pairing screen and decoupled pairing through the gateway's iOS SDK.
The result
Related support tickets dropped to zero.

Stabilizing an enterprise platform after a cloud move

Thomson Reuters · Technical Product Manager

The problem
Legal Tracker, a B2B SaaS platform serving 2,000+ clients, needed to be stabilized during its transition to Azure.
What I did
Prioritized defect resolution over new features, working across eight teams of 100+ developers and QA engineers.
The result
Legal Tracker was stable again within three to four releases, about two months.
